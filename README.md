## Project overview

FreshBasket is a fictional community-owned grocery store proposed for Thohoyandou. Community members will be able to hold shares, and the business aims to employ local youth. It plans to purchase suitable stock directly from local farmers, research prices that local customers can afford, offer selected-item coupons, and support collection and delivery enquiries.

## Website goals and objectives

1. Give customers clear information about products, selected specials, coupons and locations.
2. Make it easier to enquire about availability, collection and delivery before travelling.
3. Explain FreshBasket's community ownership, local sourcing and youth-employment goals.
4. Create a logical five-page HTML structure that can be styled in Part 2 and enhanced in Part 3.

## Key features and functionality

- Five semantic HTML pages with a consistent navigation system.
- Product categories and selected-item specials.
- Coupon programme explanation.
- Enquiry form for stock, orders, suppliers, shares and employment.
- Contact form and two proposed location references.
- Accessible labels, alternative text, headings and semantic page regions.
- Local SVG image assets with descriptive alternative text.

Online order processing, coupon validation, email processing and interactive responses are future functions planned for Part 3. The Part 1 forms currently provide the required HTML structure only.
