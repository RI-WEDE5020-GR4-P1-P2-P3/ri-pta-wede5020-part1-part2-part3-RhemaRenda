About FreshBasket

A proposed grocery store created around the needs of the Thohoyandou community.

Our proposed story

FreshBasket began as an idea after recognising the need for easier access to affordable food and reliable local product information. The store is planned as a fictional community-owned business. Community members will be able to hold shares, helping local people take part in the growth of the organisation.

The business will research suitable prices and purchase from local farmers where possible instead of depending only on large suppliers. This approach is intended to support farmers, shorten parts of the supply chain and keep more economic activity within the local area.

Mission

To make affordable food and household essentials easier to access while supporting local farmers, creating opportunities for local youth and serving customers honestly.

Vision

To become a trusted community-owned grocery store in Thohoyandou that is recognised for fair prices, fresh local products and positive community impact.

Our values

  • Community: Local people should benefit from the business.
  • Affordability: Prices and promotions should respond to customers' needs.
  • Local support: Suitable stock should be sourced from local farmers where possible.
  • Opportunity: The store should create employment pathways for local youth.
  • Honesty: Prices, coupon conditions and product information must be clear.

Who we aim to serve

FreshBasket is designed for households, students, senior citizens, working people and small local traders in and around Thohoyandou. The website will help customers check products, selected specials, coupons and collection or delivery options before travelling.

FreshBasket Neighbourhood Grocer — a fictional organisation created for the WEDE5020 POE.
