Our story

About Savanna Flame Kitchen

A proposed independent restaurant designed around the needs of Thohoyandou customers.

Why the restaurant is needed

The idea began with a simple need: customers should be able to access satisfying food throughout the day and night. Savanna Flame Kitchen plans to combine traditional South African meals with familiar fast-food choices so that different customers can find something suitable in one place.

The website will reduce uncertainty by showing menu categories, service options, opening hours, specials and contact information before customers travel or place an enquiry.

Mission

To serve accessible, dependable meals around the clock while responding to the food needs of students, workers, families, senior citizens and visitors.

Vision

To become a trusted 24-hour Thohoyandou restaurant known for local flavour, convenient service, fair value and welcoming customer care.

Our values

  • Availability: Planned 24/7 service for different routines.
  • Choice: Traditional and fast-food options.
  • Respect: Friendly service for every customer.
  • Value: Clearly explained specials and discounts.
  • Community: A locally focused Thohoyandou business.
  • Consistency: Reliable information and meal quality.

Who we aim to serve

Students need affordable options, workers may eat outside ordinary hours, families value variety, senior citizens benefit from selected discounts, and visitors need clear local information. Savanna Flame Kitchen is planned with all these customers in mind.
