# Savanna Flame Kitchen content research

## Student-supplied concept

- Location: Thohoyandou, Limpopo.
- Meal range: traditional South African food and popular fast food.
- Availability: planned 24-hour operation.
- Service choices: dine-in, collection and delivery enquiries.
- Value offers: student specials and senior-citizen discounts.
- Main purpose: make food easier to access and respond to customers' needs.

## Target audiences and needs

| Audience | Need addressed by the website |
|---|---|
| Students | Affordable selected meals and quick access to opening hours |
| Workers | Food outside ordinary meal times and collection options |
| Families | Variety across traditional and fast-food categories |
| Senior citizens | Clear information about eligible discounts |
| Visitors | Location, menu categories and contact details |
| Catering customers | A structured way to describe an event enquiry |

## Content decisions

The website does not claim that the fictional restaurant is already operating. Proposed menu items and contact details are labelled appropriately. No unconfirmed prices are invented. Part 2 concentrates on visual design and responsiveness; JavaScript behaviour and processing remain reserved for Part 3.

## Accessibility and performance

Semantic landmarks, ordered headings, labelled controls, alternative text, visible keyboard focus and readable contrast support different users. Local SVG illustrations and responsive hero images reduce unnecessary transfers and avoid relying on copyrighted photographs.
